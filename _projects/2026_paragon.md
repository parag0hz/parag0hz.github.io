### 📝 Summary

Developed Paragon during the 17th Qualcomm Institute AI Entrepreneurship Program at UC San Diego, a platform that predicts vehicle aerodynamic drag coefficients directly from 3D point clouds for real-time aerodynamic screening.

### 🛠 Tech & Role

- **Tech Stack**: PyTorch, ONNX Runtime, FastAPI, React
- **Role**: AI Engineer
- **Period**: Jun 2026 – Jul 2026

### 🔍 What I Did

- Developed 3D point-cloud regression models including PointNet and DGCNN variants to predict vehicle aerodynamic drag coefficients.
- Benchmarked deep learning and parametric ML models under a matched 5-fold protocol using R², MAE, and ranking accuracy to evaluate generalization.
- Built an end-to-end inference pipeline with PyTorch, ONNX Runtime, FastAPI, and React for real-time aerodynamic screening.
